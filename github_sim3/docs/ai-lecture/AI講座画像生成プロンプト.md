# AI体験会 企画書 イメージ画像生成プロンプト

## 1. メイン画像（体験会の雰囲気）
```
A warm photo of a small community workshop in Japan. Several middle-aged people (40-60 years old) sitting at a table, each looking at their smartphones and laptops with surprised and happy expressions. A friendly young instructor is standing and pointing at a projected screen. Bright community center room, natural lighting from windows. Warm and welcoming atmosphere. The screen shows a chat interface.
```

## 2. スマホでAIと会話している様子（案Aイメージ）
```
A close-up photo of a middle-aged Japanese person's hands holding a smartphone. The screen shows a friendly chat conversation with an AI assistant. The person is smiling. Soft warm lighting, shallow depth of field. Cozy community center setting in the background.
```

## 3. 仕事で活用するイメージ（案Bイメージ）
```
A photo of a Japanese person in their 40s-50s sitting at a desk with a laptop, looking at a screen showing AI-generated business text. They have a satisfied and impressed expression. Modern but casual setting, bright natural lighting. A printed document and coffee cup on the desk.
```
